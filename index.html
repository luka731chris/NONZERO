Concept2 PM5 connection

iPhone: use Concept2 ErgData as the Bluetooth recorder. Safari does not expose the PM5 Bluetooth stream directly to this web app.

  1. Wake the PM5.
  2. On the PM5 Main Menu choose Connect (older firmware may show More Options).
  3. Open ErgData on your iPhone and choose the PM5 shown nearby.
  4. Confirm the matching PM5 on the monitor if prompted.
  5. Start the workout only after ErgData shows connected. Keep ErgData as the one app recording the PM5.
  6. Afterward, copy the PM5/ErgData meters, watts and calories into the workout fields here; this app will label them measured instead of estimated.

On browsers with Web Bluetooth support, the experimental Connect PM5 button can attempt a direct FTMS connection.
