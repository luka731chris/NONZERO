Calendar help
iPhone/iCloud: paste the exact link produced by Calendar → Calendars → ⓘ → Public Calendar → Share Link. The planner accepts webcal://, webcals://, http://, and https://, extracts links even when surrounded by copied text, and retries Apple-specific host variants automatically.
