ONE-TIME SETUP · 4 STEPS
1. Tap CREATE MY SHORTCUT LINK.
2. Tap COPY LINK + OPEN SHORTCUTS.
3. In Shortcuts choose Automation → + → App → ErgData → Is Opened → Run Immediately.
4. Add Get Contents of URL, paste the copied link, and tap Done.
Do not change Method. Do not add Headers. Do not paste your private sync key into Shortcuts.
