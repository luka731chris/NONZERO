# Sea Pines discovery ride — v7.17.2

Replaces the Port Royal 20-mile beachfront out-and-back with a **15-mile virtual Sea Pines circuit**. Landmarks are ordered to tell a geographically coherent tour, including Sea Pines Beach Club, Atlantic shoreline, North Sea Pines Drive, Lighthouse Road, Harbour Town Golf Links, Harbour Town Lighthouse, Harbour Town Yacht Basin, Liberty Oak, Stoney-Baynard Ruins, South Sea Pines Drive, South Beach Marina Village, and Atlantic beach returns.

This is a **scenic simulation**, not a GPS-accurate track: route connecting curves, landmark anchors, and repeated beach entries are approximations. In reality the Harbour Town marina is on Calibogue Sound, and the beach segments are on the Atlantic side or at South Beach. Beach access points are represented as transitions, not asserted as verified cycling access ramps. Forest Preserve trails are not used as bike paths.

Source reference: Sea Pines 2026 leisure path map https://www.seapines.com/sites/default/files/media/documents/SPR-Bikemap-2026.pdf and community map https://www.seapinesliving.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bike-Map-2026.pdf .

No changes to PM5 telemetry, Cloudflare Worker, or phone authentication. No real Pi hardware endurance test has been performed.
