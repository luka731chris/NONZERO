# Hilton Head Port Royal beach — v7.17.1

Replaces the Cape Cod scene while retaining the `beach` scene identifier and all other six environments. The ride is a **20-mile scenic simulation**: northbound for 10 miles, then southbound for 10 miles, with the direction and turnaround exposed in route progress.

**Geographic accuracy limitation:** Port Royal Plantation does not have ten miles of beachfront in each direction. The extended distance is a repeat/interpretive scenic experience, **not a GPS-verified route within plantation property**. The existing rendering engine is stylized, not photorealistic. No Cape Cod photographs are mislabeled as Hilton Head.

PM5 recording, cloud configuration, and phone pairing are unchanged.
