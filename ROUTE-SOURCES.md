# Route sources and accuracy

The named route stops are selected from the following primary sources. The offline path curves,
Pittsburgh building footprints and most intermediate distances are simplified approximations.
The photographic chapter images preserve real skyline/landscape arrangements at their recorded viewpoints.
Photo chapters are not continuous ride footage or a claimed camera position at every meter.

- Pittsburgh: City of Pittsburgh, Allegheny Landing Park. Establishes the park between Roberto Clemente
  and Andy Warhol bridges: https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/Recreation-Events/CitiParks/Our-Parks/Allegheny-Landing-Park
  City bridge designations (6th, 7th, 9th streets):
  https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/Business-Development/City-Planning/Historic-Preservation-Program/Historic-Designations-and-Districts
- Mont Ventoux southern climb: Provence à Vélo / Ventoux Provence Tourism, Bédoin ascent:
  https://www.provence-a-velo.fr/equipements/mont-ventoux/a-ascension-du-mont-ventoux-depuis-bedoin/provence-5361302-1.html
- Rockies: NPS distance/elevation chart, Beaver Meadows column. Published miles converted to meters:
  https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/distances-and-elevations.htm
- Moab: Bureau of Land Management, Slickrock National Recreation Trail (10.5 mile loop):
  https://www.blm.gov/visit/slickrock-national-recreation-trail
  Intermediate terrain descriptors are not surveyed landmark locations.
- Cape Cod: NPS Head of the Meadow bike trail, two-mile route between High Head Road and beach:
  https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/head-of-the-meadow-bike-trail.htm

BLE protocol source:
